Clinical Pasteurisation for Vulnerable Infants

MaskPure is a medical-grade wet Holder pasteurisation platform engineered specifically for hospital neonatal intensive care units (NICUs) and human milk banks. For premature infants and medically fragile newborns, donor human milk provides vital immunological protection. MaskPure ensures every feed meets the highest standard of microbial safety while preserving the bio-compounds that help defend against neonatal complications such as necrotising enterocolitis (NEC).

Developed in the United Kingdom by MASK Control Systems Limited, MaskPure incorporates more than 25 years of advanced control systems and thermal automation engineering. The equipment executes an automated Holder pasteurisation protocol, raising donor milk to exactly 62.5 °C and maintaining that temperature for 30 minutes, followed by rapid controlled cooling. This validated thermal curve inactivates critical pathogens such as Cytomegalovirus (CMV), HIV, Hepatitis B and C, and bacterial vegetative flora without denaturing protective immunoglobulins or altering essential macronutrients.

Designed with clinical hygiene at its core, each MaskPure unit is constructed from clinical-grade brushed stainless steel for straightforward disinfection in sterile hospital environments. Units are dispatched in protective factory surface wrapping to guarantee pristine condition upon arrival. Once unwrapped in your clinical suite, the fingerprint-resistant brushed stainless finish allows easy disinfection and meets hospital infection control standards.

How MaskPure Works

A standardized 3-stage thermal process designed for clinical reliability and ease of use.

01

Secure Bottle Loading

Place donor breast milk containers into the medical-grade stainless steel basket. The chamber accommodates standard neonatal glass containers and BPA-free hospital collection bottles.

02

Automated 62.5 °C Thermal Cycle

The system brings circulating water to precisely 62.5 °C and maintains this threshold for exactly 30 minutes. Continuous micro-convection guarantees uniform thermal equilibrium throughout all bottles without manual intervention.

03

Rapid Cooling and Clinical Release

Once the holding duration finishes, an automated rapid cooling cycle brings milk temperatures down rapidly to prevent spore germination. Pasteurized milk is ready for immediate freezer storage or clinical administration.
