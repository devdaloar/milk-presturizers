What is MaskPure?

MaskPure is a wet Holder pasteurisation system built specifically for human milk banks and hospital neonatal intensive care units.

It was designed and built by MASK Control Systems Limited, a UK engineering company with more than 25 years of hands-on experience in control systems and industrial automation.

The machine heats donated breast milk to 62.5 degrees Celsius and holds it at that temperature for 30 minutes. This process removes harmful bacteria and viruses from the milk while keeping the nutritional content intact.

Premature babies and infants in hospital NICUs depend on donated breast milk. MaskPure makes sure that milk is safe before it reaches them.

How MaskPure Works

01

Load the Bottles

Place the donated breast milk bottles into the MaskPure basket system. The machine is designed to hold multiple bottles in a single cycle.

02

Pasteurisation Cycle Runs

The machine heats the milk to 62.5 degrees Celsius and holds it there for 30 minutes. The cycle is automated and controlled. No manual monitoring is needed during the process.

03

Safe Milk Ready for Use

Once the cycle is complete the milk is cooled and ready. It has been pasteurised to the accepted standard and is safe for use in your milk bank or NICU.
